Gas Balances API

The Gas Balances API provides current and historical gas balance
information.

Endpoint:
GET /api/gas-balances/{date}

The balance is calculated as:

Gas Balance = Supply - Demand

Supply consists of terminal nominations, storage withdrawals,
and imported gas.

Demand consists of industrial demand, domestic consumption,
power-generation demand, and exports.

A positive balance means supply exceeds demand.

A negative balance means demand exceeds supply.

Gas balance values are measured in GWh.
